# Marriott International Typography Standards

## Corporate Typeface

Internal corporate presentations use:
- **Primary**: Arial (cross-platform safe, always available)
- **Acceptable alternative**: Calibri (Windows), Helvetica Neue (Mac)

Do not use decorative, display, or handwritten fonts in corporate presentations.

## Type Scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deck title | 44–52pt | Regular | White (on Red/Dark bg) |
| Slide title | 24–32pt | Regular | Dark |
| Subtitle / eyebrow | 14–18pt | Regular | Gray |
| Body / bullets | 13–15pt | Regular | Dark |
| Stat values | 32–44pt | Regular | Red |
| Caption / footnote | 10–11pt | Regular | Mid Gray |

## Hierarchy rules

1. **One title per slide** — always at the top, always an insight headline
2. **No more than two text sizes on a slide** (title + body)
3. **Bold sparingly** — for emphasis within a sentence, not for entire blocks
4. **Italic sparingly** — for quotes and citations, not for body copy
5. **ALL CAPS only for labels** — section numbers, eyebrow tags (max 4 words)

## Case conventions

| Element | Convention | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Slide titles | Title Case | "AI Studio Usage Grew 40% in 90 Days" |
| Bullets | Sentence case | "Three engineers completed the 201 workshop" |
| Labels / eyebrows | ALL CAPS | "PHASE 1" |
| Captions | Sentence case | "Source: Cursor usage data, July 2026" |

## Minimum sizes

- Body text: never below 12pt
- Footnotes: never below 10pt
- If your content doesn't fit at 12pt, reduce the content, not the font
