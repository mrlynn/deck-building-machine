# Acme Corporation Typography Standards

## Corporate Typeface

Internal corporate presentations use:
- **Primary**: Arial (cross-platform safe, always available)
- **Acceptable alternative**: Calibri (Windows), Helvetica Neue (Mac)

Do not use decorative, display, or handwritten fonts in corporate presentations.

## Type Scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deck title | 44–52pt | Regular | White (on Red/Dark bg) |
| Slide title | 26–32pt | Regular | Brand Dark or Primary Red |
| Subtitle / eyebrow | 14–16pt | Regular | Corporate Gray |
| Body / bullets | 13–15pt | Regular | Brand Dark |
| Caption / footnote | 10–11pt | Regular | Mid Gray |

## Rules

- Title case for slide titles
- Sentence case for body copy and bullets
- Minimum body size: 12pt
- Do not use bold for entire paragraphs — bold only for emphasis within a sentence
- Do not use more than one typeface family per slide
