## Background

Marriott's AI Studio team (Paul Dyrwal's group) has been using Cursor through organic adoption — 1,606 seats, 1,297 active users — but with no formal rollout program. Sean Corkum, the original Cursor champion, has left the account. Paul wants to run a structured enablement program to turn his AI Studio team into Cursor power users, then use that as the model for rolling Cursor out company-wide beyond engineering to design, product, analysts, and researchers.

The program is sequenced around an August 7 meeting with Naveen. Paul needs Naveen to commit resources for the broader rollout.

---

## Key messages

1. The AI Studio team is ready to become Cursor power users — the 5-phase program shows the path from newcomers to champions
2. A sprint day will produce a concrete working prototype before the August 7 meeting, proving that Marriott can build with Cursor, not just talk about it
3. This program is the model for shifting Marriott from "deck culture" to a company-wide AI-builder culture — starting with engineering, extending to design, product, and marketing

## Content to include

### Data and metrics
- 1,606 seats purchased, 1,297 MAU (81% utilization)
- 40% MCP adoption (522 users already connected to external systems)
- 389 greenfield organic signups in the last 90 days — no formal program
- 0% Bugbot, 1.2% Cloud Agent — the depth opportunity

### Proof points
- NAB: expanded from 6,000 engineers to 10,000+ employees including product, design, and leadership
- Money Forward: 1,000+ daily users, 15–20 hours saved per developer per week
- Amplitude: 3x weekly production commits, Cloud Agents turning Slack bug reports into PRs

### Recommendation
Paul is asking Naveen to: (1) confirm resources for the sprint day, (2) extend the August 7 meeting beyond 20 minutes, and (3) add Paul to the Naveen meeting as the AI Studio sponsor

## What to avoid

- Do not make this a product pitch — Marriott already uses Cursor, this is about depth and expansion
- Keep technical Cursor features secondary — the business story (deck to prototype) is primary
