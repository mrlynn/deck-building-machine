| **Audience** | Executive sponsor + VP Engineering + AI Studio team leads |
| **Purpose** | Persuade — get exec buy-in to expand the program and confirm sprint day |

---

## Background

Acme's AI Studio team has been using Cursor through organic adoption — 1,200 seats, 950 active users — but with no formal rollout program. The original Cursor champion moved to another team. Engineering leadership wants a structured enablement program to turn the AI Studio into Cursor power users, then use that as the model for rolling Cursor out company-wide beyond engineering to design, product, analysts, and researchers.

The program is sequenced around an executive review in August. Leadership needs to commit resources for the broader rollout.

---

## Key messages

1. The AI Studio team is ready to become Cursor power users — the 5-phase program shows the path from newcomers to champions
2. A sprint day will produce a concrete working prototype before the executive review, proving that Acme can build with Cursor, not just talk about it
3. This program is the model for shifting Acme from "deck culture" to a company-wide AI-builder culture — starting with engineering, extending to design, product, and marketing

---

## Content to include

### Data and metrics
- 1,200 seats purchased, 950 MAU (79% utilization)
- 35% MCP adoption (420 users already connected to external systems)
- 280 greenfield organic signups in the last 90 days — no formal program
- Low Bugbot and Cloud Agent adoption — the depth opportunity

### Proof points
- Peer A: expanded from 6,000 engineers to 10,000+ employees including product, design, and leadership
- Peer B: 1,000+ daily users, 15–20 hours saved per developer per week
- Peer C: 3x weekly production commits, Cloud Agents turning Slack bug reports into PRs

### Recommendation
Engineering leadership is asking the executive sponsor to: (1) confirm resources for the sprint day, (2) extend the August review beyond 20 minutes, and (3) name an AI Studio sponsor for the executive meeting

---

## What to avoid

- Do not position this as an IT project or a software procurement decision
- Do not use the term "AI tool" — frame as AI-builder culture and capability
- Do not make this a product pitch — Acme already uses Cursor, this is about depth and expansion
- Keep technical Cursor features secondary — the business story (deck to prototype) is primary
